## 1. Purpose

Cash handling at a branch: tellers (a till location within an office), cashiers who work at a teller, the cash allocated to and settled by each cashier, cashier transactions, and cash transfers between offices. Every cash movement produces journal entries, so this module sits on top of the ledger. It is separate from ecb_org because organisation data is read by everything and must not depend on the ledger, while cash handling must. Fineract keeps it in its own `fineract-branch` module for the same reason.

## 2. Responsibilities

- Tellers (`m_tellers`) with status and validity dates, per office
- Cashiers (`m_cashiers`) assigned to tellers and staff, with working dates and full-day flags
- Cash allocation to and settlement from a cashier, and the cashier's transactions and summary (`m_cashier_transactions`)
- Cash transfers between offices (`m_office_transaction`)
- Journal entries for every cash movement through ecb_ledger

## 3. Out of scope

- Vault and ATM cash management beyond what Fineract models
- Customer-facing deposits and withdrawals (ecb_deposits); a teller records the cash side only

## 7. Design to follow

- Cash allocated to a cashier and cash settled back are the two directions of one flow; the cashier summary is derived from those transactions, not stored
- Each cash movement posts a journal through ecb_ledger using the financial-activity account mappings, so teller cash and the ledger never disagree
- Teller and cashier validity dates are enforced on every transaction

## 8. Build order

1. Tellers and their validity rules
2. Cashiers and assignment to staff
3. Allocate and settle cash with journals
4. Cashier summary and transactions
5. Office-to-office cash transfers

## 10. Acceptance criteria

Module-specific:

- Allocate, settle and summary results match Fineract for generated cashier days
- The cashier and teller integration classes pass

Common to every module:

- Contract probe: every `must` read operation of this module passes against the oracle (status and response shape).
- Fineract's integration-test classes listed above pass, or each failure is recorded as a divergence with an ADR.
- Every state-changing operation goes through the command bus: validated, authorised, audited, idempotent, maker-checker aware.
- Every table has an Ecto migration in the tenant repo that matches the Fineract schema, and the cross-tenant isolation test covers the module.
- Money uses `Decimal` with an explicit currency and rounding rule; no floats.
- Dependencies point only at the modules in the header. `mix xref` finds no cycle or reverse dependency.
- `mix format`, Credo strict, Dialyzer and the module's tests pass; the module README and this document are updated.
