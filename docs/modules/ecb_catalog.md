## 1. Purpose

The building blocks that products are assembled from: charges (fees and penalties, with calculation types and due-date rules), tax components and groups, rates, floating rates, and the interest-rate charts with their slabs and incentives that fixed and recurring deposits use. Deposits and loans refer to these definitions but do not own them.

## 2. Responsibilities

- Charges (`m_charge`) with applies-to, calculation type, time type, currency, tax group and the active flag
- Tax components with history and tax groups with their mappings (`m_tax_component`, `m_tax_group`)
- Rates (`m_rate`) and floating rates with periods (`m_floating_rates`, `m_floating_rates_periods`)
- Interest-rate charts, slabs and incentives (`m_interest_rate_chart`, `m_interest_rate_slab`, `m_interest_incentives`)

## 3. Out of scope

- Applying a charge to an account (ecb_deposits, ecb_loans)
- Tax calculation on a transaction (done by the product that posts it, using these definitions)

## 7. Design to follow

- Charges have a calculation type (flat, percent of amount, percent of interest, and so on) and a time type (disbursement, specified due date, installment fee, overdue, and so on); the enumerations and their numeric codes must match Fineract because clients send and receive the codes
- Tax components keep a history of percentage changes with start and end dates; a charge points at a tax group, and the tax applies as of the transaction date
- Interest-rate chart slabs are bands by term and amount; incentives modify the rate for a client attribute
- The charge 'template' endpoint returns option lists; the Mifos console builds forms from it, so its shape is part of the contract

## 8. Build order

1. Charges with the enumerations and the template endpoint
2. Tax components and groups with history
3. Rates and floating rates
4. Interest-rate charts, slabs, incentives (needed before deposits products)

## 10. Acceptance criteria

Module-specific:

- Enumeration codes and template option lists match the oracle byte for byte in shape
- Tax-component history lookups return the same component percentage as Fineract for a date corpus
- The charge, tax and rate integration classes pass

Common to every module:

- Contract probe: every `must` read operation of this module passes against the oracle (status and response shape).
- Fineract's integration-test classes listed above pass, or each failure is recorded as a divergence with an ADR.
- Every state-changing operation goes through the command bus: validated, authorised, audited, idempotent, maker-checker aware.
- Every table has an Ecto migration in the tenant repo that matches the Fineract schema, and the cross-tenant isolation test covers the module.
- Money uses `Decimal` with an explicit currency and rounding rule; no floats.
- Dependencies point only at the modules in the header. `mix xref` finds no cycle or reverse dependency.
- `mix format`, Credo strict, Dialyzer and the module's tests pass; the module README and this document are updated.
