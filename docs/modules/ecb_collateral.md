## 1. Purpose

Collateral definitions and collateral held against clients and loans, and guarantors with their funding details and transactions. It is kept apart from loans so lending stays focused, and because collateral valuation and guarantor funding are separate concerns with their own lifecycle. Its operations are called from loan commands.

## 2. Responsibilities

- Collateral management definitions (`m_collateral_management`) with quality, unit type and pct to base
- Client collateral and loan collateral (`m_client_collateral_management`, `m_loan_collateral_management`, `m_loan_collateral`)
- Guarantors, their funding details and guarantor transactions (`m_guarantor`, `m_guarantor_funding_details`, `m_guarantor_transaction`), including hold on funds and release
- Collateral value checks that loan approval and disbursement call

## 3. Out of scope

- Valuation workflow and legal perfection of security: surrounding systems
- The loan itself (ecb_loans)

## 7. Design to follow

- In Fineract the guarantor code lives inside the loan account package; we move it here and expose a narrow interface to ecb_loans, a deliberate improvement on its entanglement
- Guarantor funding places a hold on the guarantor's savings account through ecb_deposits and releases or consumes it on loan events

## 8. Build order

1. Collateral management definitions and client collateral
2. Loan collateral and the value check
3. Guarantors and funding, after ecb_deposits holds exist
