## 1. Purpose

Savings and current accounts, fixed deposits and recurring deposits, with their products, charges, transactions, interest calculation and posting, holds, dormancy, account transfers and standing instructions. It is the first product module and the one a retail bank needs for CASA. Interest and balance arithmetic here must match Fineract to the cent.

## 2. Responsibilities

- Savings products and accounts (`m_savings_product`, `m_savings_account`), with the account lifecycle (submit, approve, activate, reject, withdraw, close)
- Deposits, withdrawals, holds and releases, overdraft, minimum balance, dormancy and inactivity rules
- Interest: calculation types (daily balance, average daily balance), compounding, posting frequency, and the interest posting job that ecb_batch runs
- Savings charges (`m_savings_account_charge`) and the tax on interest
- Fixed deposit products and accounts, with term, pre-closure and maturity instructions; recurring deposits with mandatory schedules (`m_mandatory_savings_schedule`)
- Account transfers and standing instructions, with their run history
- Savings transaction search and the transaction datatables

## 3. Out of scope

- Interest-rate chart definitions and charges (ecb_catalog)
- Journal posting rules (ecb_ledger)
- The scheduled interest posting job runner (ecb_batch)
- Group savings (deferred)

## 7. Design to follow

- Savings transactions are immutable rows (`m_savings_account_transaction`) with `is_reversed`, `is_reversal` and `original_transaction_id`; an undo creates a reversal, never an update
- Each transaction stores derived running-balance fields (`running_balance_derived`, `cumulative_balance_derived`, `balance_end_date_derived`); balances are recomputed in date order when a back-dated transaction arrives, so the recalculation routine is the core of this module
- Interest posting is its own documented flow (`features/savings-interest-posting.adoc`), including posting on the business date and compounding periods
- Business date, not wall-clock time, decides the transaction date when the client gives none
- A deposit is a hold-aware balance: holds (`m_deposit_account_on_hold_transaction`) reduce available balance without moving money
- Account transfers move money between savings and loan accounts as a pair of transactions inside one command

## 8. Build order

1. Savings product definition and the account lifecycle
2. Deposit and withdrawal with running balances and reversal
3. Back-dated transaction recalculation
4. Interest calculation and posting (pure functions first, then the posting command)
5. Charges, tax on interest, holds, dormancy
6. Fixed deposits, then recurring deposits
7. Account transfers and standing instructions

## 10. Acceptance criteria

Module-specific:

- For a generated corpus of savings accounts with back-dated deposits, withdrawals and reversals, balances, running balances and interest match Fineract exactly
- Journals produced for each scenario match Fineract's under cash and accrual accounting
- The savings, fixed deposit, recurring deposit, account transfer and standing instruction integration classes pass

Common to every module:

- Contract probe: every `must` read operation of this module passes against the oracle (status and response shape).
- Fineract's integration-test classes listed above pass, or each failure is recorded as a divergence with an ADR.
- Every state-changing operation goes through the command bus: validated, authorised, audited, idempotent, maker-checker aware.
- Every table has an Ecto migration in the tenant repo that matches the Fineract schema, and the cross-tenant isolation test covers the module.
- Money uses `Decimal` with an explicit currency and rounding rule; no floats.
- Dependencies point only at the modules in the header. `mix xref` finds no cycle or reverse dependency.
- `mix format`, Credo strict, Dialyzer and the module's tests pass; the module README and this document are updated.
